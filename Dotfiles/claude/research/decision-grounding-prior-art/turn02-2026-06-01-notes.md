## Findings (lifted, one-line)
- CONVERGENT THESIS across all 3 sub-classes: each substitutes a SELF-REFERENTIAL evaluation signal for an
  external ground-truth anchor — DR agents self-cross-check citations (and hallucinate them anyway); Sakana
  optimizes "passes our own LLM reviewer"; co-scientist optimizes an Elo from Gemini-debating-Gemini. The ONLY
  artifacts in the corpus that PASS the acid test are the external CRITIQUE papers, never the systems.
- Deep-research agents (OpenAI/Gemini/Perplexity): uniformly retrieve→reason→synthesize→cite with an ADAPTIVE
  plan that forms hypotheses *from* the data = the inverse of pre-registration. No gold set, no exploratory/
  confirmatory split, "adversarial" = self-check that empirically fails. Class graded F as decision-grounding
  prior art. Best partial precedent: Gemini "collaborative planning" (scopes the question; stops short of anchor).
- Hard numbers on the grounding failure (`A-reference-hallucination-dr`, grade A): 3–13% of citation URLs are
  hallucinated (never existed), 5–18% non-resolving; DR agents hallucinate MORE than plain search-augmented
  LLMs (pooled 10.7% vs 4.8%); gemini-2.5-pro-deepresearch worst at 13.3%; some OpenAI models fabricate EVERY
  non-resolving URL; a `urlhealth` self-correction tool cuts it to <1% (when tool-use is competent). This paper
  itself PASSES the acid test (pre-stated RQs, defined population, Wayback ground-truth oracle, bootstrap CIs,
  adversarial self-audit) — the model of the discipline our skill wants.
- Sakana AI-Scientist (v1 grade D / v2 C−): success metric is "passes our LLM reviewer" / clears a high-accept
  (60–70%) ICLR *workshop*, never "matches a held-out gold set / survives adversarial correctness check." v2
  ships validation knobs (`k_fold_validation`, `expose_prediction`) that are EXPLICITLY DISABLED. "First
  peer-reviewed paper" = 1 of 3 accepted at a workshop with NO meta-review, withdrawn by pre-agreed design;
  Sakana's own team said none passed their conference bar.
- Independent Sakana critique (`A-sakana-ai-scientist-critique`, grade A−): ran v1 → ~half experiments fail to
  execute; 4/7 manuscripts contain hallucinated numerical results; no CIs/p-values; novelty-check is keyword
  matching (labeled all 12 ideas "novel" despite known prior art); it "failed to re-run the baseline, making
  its novel approach appear superior — an impossible outcome"; self-reviewer rejected 9/10 real OpenReview
  papers incl. 4 that were accepted. n=1 domain but reproducible + authors are AI-optimists (so the negative
  findings disconfirm their own priors → more credible).
- Google AI co-scientist (blog D / arXiv 2502.18864 C− / Nature C): self-improvement optimizes an Elo the
  authors REPEATEDLY disclaim as "not based on an independent ground truth" and warn "may favour results that
  do not … align with … accuracy." "Learning without back-propagation" = appending critiques to prompts. Real
  external evidence is a thin wet-lab layer: 1 genuinely-novel AML in-vitro hit (KIRA6); liver-fibrosis deferred
  to "an upcoming report"; AMR/cf-PICI is RE-derivation of the team's own pre-existing unpublished answer (zero
  new validation). Nature peer-review NARROWED the abstract to foreground only AML as validated; Nature ran an
  editorial "Why AI cannot do good science without humans."
- DESIGN IMPLICATIONS for our skill (carry to stages 2–3):
  1. Give any internal critic / LLM-judge / ensemble-vote ZERO ground-truth credit. The skill must force naming
     an EXTERNAL anchor (gold set / held-out outcome / pre-registered prediction / real-world adjudication)
     committed BEFORE the data.
  2. The "clean-context adversarial stage" (design property B) is right to use a DIFFERENT context — but per the
     refined CLAUDE.md it "buys breadth, not truth — verify survivors." The skill must NOT claim its adversarial
     stage confers truth; only breadth + a human-verification step.
  3. The acid test gates on the FORM-vs-SUBSTANCE gap: an artifact can emit the entire form of science
     (hypotheses, experiments, figures, citations, even real peer-review acceptance) with no anchoring mechanism.
