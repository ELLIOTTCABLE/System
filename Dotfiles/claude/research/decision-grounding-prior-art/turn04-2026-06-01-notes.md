Bucket-(a) wave 1: the pre-commitment family (survey spine) + bias-control-and-its-limits (self-critique headline).

## Findings (lifted, one-line)
- PRE-COMMITMENT is one mechanism independently reconverged across ~5 fields / ~130 years; the early nodes are
  genuinely independent (strong evidence it's real, not citation-fashion): Chamberlin (geology 1890/97) →
  Platt (general method 1964, explicitly fuses Bacon+Chamberlin) ; Tukey (stats EDA/CDA 1962/77, contemporaneous
  with Platt, no cross-cite) ; Klein-Roodman (particle physics blind analysis 2005, lineage = speed-of-light
  clustering + "Clever Hans", not the others) ; Gelman-Loken (forking paths 2013) → Nosek (preregistration
  synthesis 2018, which itself enumerates the cross-disciplinary synonyms).
- DISTILLED MECHANISM (survey spine): separate GENERATING an explanation from TESTING it; lock the testing
  commitments (which hypotheses are in play + what evidence counts for/against each) BEFORE seeing the deciding
  data; hold the candidates as a SET you are equally willing to kill, not one pet belief you defend.
- MINIMAL ENGINEER VERSION (1hr–1day): (1) write 2–4 candidate answers incl. the boring null/status-quo;
  (2) for each, pre-state the observation that would KILL it (Platt's "what would disprove this?"); (3) write the
  exact metric/query + decision rule before running, incl. IF-THEN branches (COS "decision-tree"); (4) if you must
  explore first, SPLIT the data — explore one slice, confirm the frozen hypothesis on a held-out slice; label
  exploratory findings (COS split-sample; Tukey EDA→CDA); (5) if possible BLIND yourself to the answer while
  fixing the procedure (Klein-Roodman hidden-offset/signal-box — the most engineer-shaped tactic); (6) report ALL
  pre-committed candidates incl. the losers (Gelman-Loken anti-forking-path).
- CRUCIAL TWO-TRADITION DISTINCTION (reshapes the skill's value prop):
  · Tradition 1 = structural pre-commitment / blinding / multiple-hypotheses (A1). Works by CONSTRUCTION — if the
    analysis is fixed before the data, the data cannot be retro-fitted. This is a logical/structural guarantee,
    NOT a "checklist debiases humans" empirical claim. Better-grounded. (Still: preregistration's real-world
    efficacy is debated — stay humble.)
  · Tradition 2 = debiasing CHECKLISTS as training (A2, the SAT/ACH tradition). Empirically UNVALIDATED, partly
    INVALIDATED. Our skill must lean on Tradition 1 and be skeptical of Tradition 2.
- SELF-CRITIQUE, now EMPIRICALLY GROUNDED (A2): the SAT category is UNVALIDATED; the one SAT actually RCT-tested
  (ACH) tips toward partially INVALIDATED — tested, found not to help, signs of harm.
  · Smoking gun = Dhami/Belton/Mandel 2019 (Applied Cognitive Psychology, OPEN ACCESS, RCT, n=50 practicing UK
    intel analysts, ACH-trained vs control, known Bayesian ground truth): no accuracy gain (36% vs 33% correct,
    χ² p=.845, φ=.03); "may increase judgement inconsistency and error"; ACH worsened tie-rates (80% vs 19% tied,
    p<.001); ACH analysts LESS likely to use base rates (12% vs 52%, p=.002); fidelity collapse — only 20% used
    ACH's defining step. Authors: "consider alternatives" given "the paucity of research supporting the efficacy of ACH."
  · Chang/Berdini/Mandel/Tetlock 2018 (peer-reviewed, Inman award): "SATs have not been subject to sustained
    scientific testing of the sort that could reveal when they are helping or harming"; decomposition "may add noise."
  · Doctrine's own authors concede: Heuer — awareness of bias "does little by itself to help"; CIA Tradecraft
    Primer — techniques are "no guarantee of analytic precision or accuracy," only "sophistication and credibility."
- OUR EXPOSURE + DEFENSE (load-bearing for README caveat + skill self-doubt threading):
  · Cannot inherit "structured technique ∴ more rigorous" — that's exactly what the literature falsifies.
  · Two caveats partly favor an LLM impl: (1) an LLM follows a procedure with far higher FIDELITY than humans
    (the 80%-non-compliance failure mode may not transfer); (2) the better-validated debiasing interventions
    (Morewedge feedback/practice games — effect persisted ≥2 months; Bayesian info-structuring; consider-the-
    opposite) share features an LLM workflow can cheaply implement.
  · HONEST FRAMING: build as a noise/bias-AWARE, disconfirmation-and-calibration-oriented workflow whose conceded
    value is AUDITABILITY/TRANSPARENCY (the one benefit even critics grant) + structural pre-commitment (Tradition
    1), explicitly NOT a validated debiaser; treat the skill as a TESTABLE artifact (ground-truth eval), not an
    efficacy claim.
