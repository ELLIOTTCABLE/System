## Findings (lifted, one-line)
- ROUTING spine = Cynefin (A, Snowden-Boone HBR 2007): diagnose the cause/effect structure, THEN apply the
  domain-appropriate response. clear→sense-categorize-respond(rule); complicated→sense-analyze-respond(expert,
  range of right answers); complex→probe-sense-respond(safe-to-fail experiments; "cause and effect can only be
  deduced in retrospect… no right answers" → anchors BOTH causal-REFUSE and exploratory-as-provisional);
  chaotic→act-sense-respond; disorder→"break down… and assign each to one of the other four" (literally the router).
- RIGOR DIAL = Amazon one-way/two-way door (A): irreversible→deep analysis; reversible→act on ~70% of data
  ("waiting for 90%… moving too slow"); A/B test = canonical two-way door. Rigor scales to irreversibility.
- REFUSAL-TYPE ANCHORS verified:
  · causal (REFUSE) ← Hammerton & Munafò 2021 (A, Psych Medicine OA): individual causal effects unidentifiable
    from observational data ("missing data problem"); confounding/selection/measurement can flip the estimate;
    "No single study or method… can provide a definite answer to a causal question." Escape hatch = needs DESIGN
    (experiment/natural-experiment/triangulation w/ different bias directions) — which a pure corpus lacks.
  · values/definitional (NOT-MEASURABLE) ← Gallie 1956 essentially-contested (B, grading-cert -1, primary PDF
    unreadable): disputes "not resolvable by argument"; contestation at the CORE; + discriminator checklist
    (catachresis/homonymy/resolvable? vs truly-contested) to avoid OVER-refusing. Operational gate = McHugh κ
    (A): κ<0.60 = inadequate agreement ("about half the data may be incorrect"); need ≥30 comparisons; κ can
    UNDER-state → report κ AND % agreement, don't over-refuse on κ alone.
  · already-answered ← Ganann rapid reviews 2010 (A, BMC OA): time-boxed synthesis legit IF methods stated +
    conclusions interim + DON'T drop quality assessment (costliest shortcut); priority quality > breadth.
  · estimation ← (A4) Hubbard + GJP. inaccessible-population ← (A4) Shadish external-validity + (A3) selection
    bias. [A3 GAP: types 2 & 6 not anchored within A3's own set — covered by A4; type 6 is the WEAKEST-anchored,
    classic anchor would be survey-methodology coverage-error — soft gap, note in survey.]
- ESTIMATION protocol = Hubbard AIE (A-, legit summary): measurement = uncertainty reduction; 5-step (define
  decision+variables → calibrated 90% CIs → Value-of-Information rank → measure highest-VoI only, re-loop →
  decide); Fermi decompose; equivalent-bet calibration; measurement inversion; "measure just enough" (~2-10% of
  EVPI); Rule of Five (93.75% median within min-max of 5 samples).
- THE ONE RCT-BACKED PIECE = GJP/superforecasting (A, AI Impacts + fs.blog): 1-hour training improved accuracy,
  PERSISTED ≥1yr (RCT → calibration IS trainable); aggregated beat control >60%, beat classified-access analysts
  25-30%; skill is a trait (0.65 yr-over-yr corr; ~70% retain); reference-class forecasting = highest-yield
  component; granularity is real signal. Limitation: geopolitical, months-to-2yr horizon; generalization unproven.
- ENGINEER BRIDGES (both unvalidated-but-legible): GQM (B+, goal→question→metric; only collect data that answers
  a question serving a goal; never RCT'd) + EBSE Kitchenham (B+, the "Five A's" Ask/Acquire/Appraise/Apply/Assess
  from evidence-based medicine; SLR cornerstone; SE evidence weaker than medicine — skill factor, no Cochrane).
- TRANSFERABLE VALIDITY CHECKLIST = Shadish-Cook-Campbell (A-): statistical-conclusion (effect real + uncertainty
  honest?) / internal (causal or confounded?) / construct (measuring & naming the right thing?) / external
  (generalizes to where I'll apply it?). Durable/ubiquitous classification, NOT an effect-size intervention.
- SELF-CRITIQUE EXTENDS BEYOND SATs (A4 part c, important): real empirical backing exists ONLY for GJP/calibration
  (controlled effect sizes). Hubbard AIE = decision-theoretically sound but practitioner-anecdote numbers.
  GQM / EBSE / validity-typology = sensible-sounding scaffolding, valuable as engineer-legible structure, NOT
  shown to improve outcomes by N%. ⇒ the honest stance: the skill rests on (1) STRUCTURAL pre-commitment (sound
  BY CONSTRUCTION, not RCT — A1), (2) calibration/forecasting (RCT-backed — GJP), (3) a lot of sensible-but-
  unvalidated scaffolding whose conceded value is auditability/transparency, not proven debiasing.
