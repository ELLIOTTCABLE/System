## Findings (lifted, one-line)
- The acid-test mechanism the prompt wants ("anchor ground truth + commit analysis before seeing data")
  is NOT one idea — it's a convergent family across ≥4 independent disciplines: **preregistration**
  (psych/social), **blind analysis** (particle physics), **exploratory-vs-confirmatory** (Tukey/stats),
  **strong inference / multiple working hypotheses** (Platt/Chamberlin). Strong triangulation that the
  acid test is real methodology, not the prompt-author's invention.
- The prompt's refusal-typology maps cleanly onto existing literature spines:
  already-answered→rapid/systematic review; estimation→calibrated estimation (Hubbard)/forecasting (Tetlock);
  exploratory→Tukey EDA (label hypotheses, no rigor conferred); causal→causal-inference/RCT (corpus can't);
  values/definitional→essentially-contested-concepts (Gallie) + low inter-rater reliability (kappa);
  inaccessible-population→external validity / sampling frame (Shadish-Cook-Campbell).
- Bucket (b) splits into 3 sub-classes: (i) **deep-research agents** (OpenAI/Gemini/Perplexity) — retrieval+synthesis,
  no frame/gold-set/preregistration; (ii) **automated scientists** (Sakana AI-Scientist v1/v2, Google AI co-scientist) —
  claim hypothesis→experiment→paper loop; (iii) **reasoning/prompt patterns** (ReAct/Reflexion/CoV/ToT) — and
  the **agent-skills ecosystem** (Anthropic SKILL.md, obra/superpowers, claudeskills.org).
- Independent skeptical ammunition already located for the form-vs-substance critique:
  Potemkin-understanding (ICML 2025), reference-hallucination-in-DR-agents (arXiv 2026), Nature on AI-invalid
  citations polluting literature, RAND+Chang critiques that even *human* SATs lack proof they reduce bias,
  generalcompute "each paper = a name + a prompt template + a benchmark table" (provenance TBD).
- Caution flag for our own skill: the SAT critique (RAND RR1408; Chang et al. 2018) says structured *human*
  debiasing techniques are largely unvalidated — i.e. "structure-shaped" ≠ "bias-reducing". Our skill must
  not commit the same sin it accuses (b) of. This is the central design risk to carry into stages 2-3.
