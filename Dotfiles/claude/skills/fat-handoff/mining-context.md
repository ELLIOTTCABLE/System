# Mining your own context

You haven't been ledgering, so the handoff has to carry what a ledger would have. In a long session you recall the middle worst; don't work from your sense of its gist.

Walk the session in order, a stretch at a time. In each, find what the human ruled, corrected, asked for and deferred, and what was decided, tried and dropped, with why.

The human's own words weigh most. Quote them where the wording matters, and say which the human typed, which they acked, and which are your paraphrase, using the project's own tags for that if it has any.
